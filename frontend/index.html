Deep Learning Architecture & Model Specifications

Graph Encoder
3× GATv2Conv layers with 4 attention heads and edge attribute integration (Dim: 37 → 64×4 → 64×4 → 128).
Fingerprint MLP
Morgan ECFP4 (2048 bits, radius 2) passed through 2048 → 256 → 128 MLP with Dropout(0.1).
Descriptors MLP
12 RDKit descriptors (MolWt, LogP, TPSA, HBD, HBA, Rings, etc.) passed through 12 → 64 → 32 MLP.
Symmetric Pair Trunk
Symmetric fusion: [r1+r2, r1*r2, |r1-r2|] (768 dims) → 256 dims MLP. Guarantees mathematical symmetry f(A,B) == f(B,A).
Calibration & Gate
Temperature Platt Scaling (T = 1.1231) with decision threshold τ = 0.5100 on validation split.
Rule-Based Clinical Risk
Clinical severity tiering (None, Low, Moderate, High) derived directly from active interaction types and quantile cutoffs.
Hierarchical Clinical Prescribing Head
4-tier clinical action head (Safe, Spacing, Dose Adjust, Contraindicated) conditioned on trunk embeddings + 40 mechanism probabilities (296 → 128 → 4).
Training Cohort
Trained across 645 curated PubChem drug compounds and verified clinical interactions.
